About the workshop

The AI and Intelligent Transportation Systems Workshop is a one-day research event bringing together faculty, students, and invited researchers from UW–Madison, Texas A&M University, and Marquette University.

The workshop will feature three invited research talks covering emerging topics in AI, autonomy, computer vision, and intelligent transportation systems, followed by a poster and research exchange session. The event is intended to facilitate academic exchange, student engagement, and potential research collaborations across related disciplines.

Faculty organizers

Prof. Xiaopeng Li

UW–Madison

Xiaopeng Li is the Harvey D. Spangler Professor in Civil and Environmental Engineering at UW–Madison. His research combines modeling and field experiments on connected, electric, and automated vehicles with transportation and infrastructure systems analysis. He directs the USDOT Rural Autonomous Vehicle Program for People Movement.

Faculty profile — Xiaopeng Li

Prof. Sue Ahn

UW–Madison

Sue Ahn is a Professor of Civil and Environmental Engineering at UW–Madison. She studies traffic flow theory and operations, intelligent transportation systems, and the environmental and safety effects of traffic. Her work uses observation, experimentation, and quantitative methods to develop traffic theories and control strategies.
